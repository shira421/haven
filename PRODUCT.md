# Haven: what is built

This file says what Haven does today and what it does not, so nothing is taken for built that is not.

## Who it is for

People who study or work in focused blocks and want a reason to stay off their phone, alone or with friends, and who would rather look after something alive than tick off a timer.

## Built

### Focusing

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Timer | 5 to 180 min in 5 min steps. Cancel free in the first 10 s. |
| Stopwatch | Counts up. Grows something from 10 min, stops by itself at 180 min. |
| Pomodoro | Focus, short break, long break and rounds are all adjustable. The next round can start by itself. |
| Deep focus | Away from Haven for longer than the allowance (30 s to 2 min, 30 s to start with) and the session fails. Can be turned off. |
| Come back | A third of the way through the allowance (10 s of 30 s), a notification asks you to come back before the session is lost. Not sent when the phone was only locked. |
| Lock is not leaving | The screen may turn off during a session, as it normally would. "Keep the screen on" is off unless you turn it on. The apps tell a locked phone from a switched app themselves. A phone's browser is told only "hidden" either way, and a locked screen must never cost a session, so it judges on return from what the leaving left behind and counts only time plainly spent in another app: time the phone slept never counts (the browser's monotonic clock stops in sleep), a swipe to another app is seen in the touch it cancels, and on Chrome for Android Haven can be allowed to know when the screen locks (Idle Detection). Anything it cannot tell is taken as a locked screen. Settings shows how the last absence was judged. It sends a come-back notification only after a swipe out. On a computer's browser, leaving counts as before. |
| Tags | Up to 12, each with a colour, shown in the journal. |
| Sounds | Seven background sounds and a finishing chime, generated on the device. |
| Session survives a restart | A running session is stored and resumes, or is judged by how long Haven was closed. |

### The living haven

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Free roaming | Animals walk, hop, crawl or fly anywhere on the land. Nothing is tied to a tile. |
| Drawn to move | Every animal is drawn from the side with its own legs, head, ears, tail and wings. Legs stride in pairs, wings flap, tails wag, eyes blink, chests breathe. |
| Weight | Walkers gather speed and slow to a stop, and go round rocks, trees and water. Hops rise and fall with the body tipping through them. Birds walk short distances and fly long ones. The shadow stays on the ground. |
| Doing things | Swimming (sunk to the belly, with ripples), drinking at the bank, grazing, resting by the fire, hiding in the burrow, perching, landing on flowers, running when called or chased. |
| Effects | A splash on entering water, dust when a big hop lands, leaves when something lands in a tree, plants rustling when brushed past. |
| Calling | Tap the ground and up to three animals nearby come over. Fed animals stop to eat; petted ones jump. |
| Likes | Each animal visits the plants and nature it likes: 29 sets of likes across flowers, trees, cover, water and built things. |
| Into and onto things | Swimmers go into the pond. Perchers sit up in trees and on the bird house. Fliers hover over flowers. What is visited rustles. |
| Each other | Animals walk up to greet each other and play chase. |
| Day and night | After 6:30 pm day animals sleep where they are. Owls, foxes, raccoons, hedgehogs and fennecs stay up. The campfire and the lantern glow. |
| Treats | Every finished session earns one, and one more per 15 min. You start with three. |
| Feeding | An animal is full for 6 hours after eating, then peckish, and hungry after 20. Feed one, or everyone who will eat. |
| Treats and pats | Put a treat down on the land and the hungriest awake animal fetches it; a full one only sniffs. Tap an animal to pat it, or stroke it; every third pat in a row delights it. A pat wakes a sleeping animal at night, which yawns and falls back asleep when left alone. |
| Petting | Any time. It counts toward the bond once an hour. |
| Bond | Five levels. Each new level pays coins. Feeding a hungry animal bonds a little more. |
| Names | Any animal can be named. |
| Dog breeds | Mixed, shiba, golden retriever, corgi, husky, dalmatian, dachshund. Picked in the care sheet. Shibas from before 1.3 are dogs of the shiba breed. |
| No punishment | A hungry animal is slow, stays close and asks for food. It never leaves, sickens or dies. |
| Arranging | Drag anything to any point on the land, put it in storage, or let it go. |

### Growing

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| 29 plants and 29 animals | Original drawings. Four growth stages during a session. Three sizes by session length. |
| 10 nature items | Tall grass, tree stump, boulder, mossy log, burrow, bird house, beehive, stone lantern, campfire, pond. Each needs a session of 10 to 60 min or more. |
| Coins | 2 a minute, more for longer sessions, streaks and friends. |
| Unlocks | Coins, goals (for example a 120 min session for the bonsai) or free in season. Nothing costs money. |
| Levels and land | Level from total focused time. The land grows from room for 25 things to room for 100. |
| Grows where planted | What a session makes appears at its spot from the first minute and stays there. |
| 29 achievements | Each pays coins. |

### Friends

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Haven opens on a sign-in screen. An account is a username and a password (at least 8 characters), plus the name friends see. Where the server offers it, "Continue with Google" signs in without a password; a new person then chooses a username. Settings changes the password (which signs other devices out), connects or disconnects Google, signs out and deletes the account. |
| Accounts from before 1.4 | Had no password. On opening Haven they must set one before anything else; it is tied to the username they already have, and their haven and friends stay. |
| Adding friends | By friend code, exact username or invite link. Requests, accept, decline, cancel, remove, block. |
| Live presence | Online, focusing and time left, today's minutes. Arrives over a live connection. |
| Cheer and nudge | Delivered at once, or when the friend is next online. |
| Gifts | Send a plant, animal or nature item you have unlocked. It arrives in their haven. |
| Visit | See a friend's haven and totals. |
| Campfire | Up to 8 people. Lit from Friends, or for a whole sanctuary at once. Start together on the server's clock. "The fire goes out for everyone" or "leaving costs only them". |
| Board | You and your friends for today, week, month and all time. |
| Activity | What you and your friends grew in the last 14 days. |
| Recovery | On Android, if the system stops the page inside the app, Haven reloads it instead of closing. If Haven does close unexpectedly, it says so the next time it opens and offers the details to copy for a bug report. A running session is kept either way. |
| Other devices | Signing in with the username and password (or Google) on another phone or computer brings the haven with it, behind a loading screen. One-time device codes are gone. Signing out saves the haven to the account and takes it off the device. After signing in every change on one device reaches the others: within seconds when they are open, otherwise when they open. Changes made on two devices at once are joined, not overwritten. |
| Sanctuaries | Up to 8 havens, joined into one land around a clearing with a great tree, with stepping stones where two meet, and animals cross freely between them. The tree grows with the week's focus (2 h per haven, at least 4 h) and blooms; it starts again each Monday. Treats and pats for anyone's animals, and their people are told. Animals from different havens become pals. Each member can be added as a friend, reported or blocked. A loading screen while the land is fetched and joined. |
| Notifications | Asked for once, at the first session. Settings shows when they are off, with a way to turn them on. Session finished, come back, friend requests and acceptances, campfire and sanctuary invitations, care from sanctuary members, pals and the tree in bloom, cheers, nudges and gifts, streak about to end (8 pm), hungry animals (by day), daily reminder. Each can be switched off in Settings. |
| Backup | To the server when signed in, or as a file. |

## Not built

- **Per-app allow list.** Letting chosen apps through needs Apple's Family Controls entitlement on iOS and usage-access permission with a background service on Android. Haven forgives a set time away instead.
- **Planting real trees.**
- **Animals in a friend's haven do not walk about** on a visit. They do in a sanctuary.
- **Animals do not interact with the session being grown**, and they do not breed, age or have different foods. There is one kind of treat.
- **With reduced motion switched on in the phone's settings, animals stay still.** Care still works.
- **Widgets, watch apps, and scheduled blocking.**
- **Instant push from the server.** There is no Apple or Google push service behind Haven. While the app is closed, the phone checks for friends' news about every 15 minutes on Android, and when iOS allows it on iPhone (often less often). Session, come-back, streak, hunger and daily reminders are scheduled on the phone and arrive on time.
- **Sounds while the phone is locked.**
- **App Store and Play Store listings.** The apps are installed from the release page.
- **Languages other than English.**

## Limits to know

- The Haven server is one Durable Object. It suits a circle of friends up to a few thousand people. See ARCHITECTURE.md.
- Coins, treats, bonds and the haven live on your devices, with a copy on the server that keeps them in step. The server checks session times for the board, not coins.
- A sanctuary shows each haven as its owner last shared it. A treat or pat given there reaches the animal's owner as a note; their bond grows only from their own care.
- Where an animal is wandering is not saved. On opening the app each starts from where it was last put down.
- Haven has no email address for anyone, so a forgotten password cannot be reset by email. Connecting Google gives a second way in; otherwise whoever runs the server can clear the password of a username (`UPDATE users SET pass=NULL WHERE handle='…'` in the Durable Object's SQL), after which a device that is still signed in is asked to set a new one.
- Without a server (a copy of Haven built with none) there are no accounts and no sign-in screen; the haven lives on the device only.
