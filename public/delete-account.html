Haven

Delete your Haven account

This removes your account, your haven, your sessions, your friends, your messages and the reports you made. It happens straight away and cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for details.

In the app

Open Haven, tap Settings at the top right, and choose Delete account at the end of the Account section.

Here, with your username and password

Your account is deleted. Haven no longer keeps anything about you, apart from reports others made about an account, as the privacy policy says.

If you sign in with Google

Open Haven on the web, choose Continue with Google, then go to Settings and choose Delete account. If you cannot sign in at all, write to the contact email on Haven's Google Play page from any address, with your username, and the account will be deleted within 30 days.
