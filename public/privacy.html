Haven

Privacy policy

Haven is a focus timer. You focus, and a plant grows or an animal moves in. You can focus with friends and share a sanctuary with them. This page says what Haven keeps about you and why.

Last updated 18 November 2026. Applies to the Haven apps for Android and iPhone and the website at shira421.github.io/haven.

Who runs Haven

Haven is made and run by one developer, shira421. To ask about your data, use the contact email on Haven's Google Play page.

What Haven keeps

You need an account to use Haven. The server keeps:

  • Your account: your username, display name and the animal you picked as your picture. Your password is kept only as a salted hash, never as you typed it.
  • Google, if you use it: Google's id for your account and the name Google gives, so you can sign in that way. Haven does not get your email address or contacts.
  • Your haven: the plants and animals you grew, where they stand, your coins and treats, so it looks the same on every device.
  • Your sessions: when each session started, how long it was, what you grew and whether you finished.
  • Friends and sanctuaries: who your friends are, requests, people you blocked, the sanctuaries you are in, and campfires you joined.
  • Messages between people: cheers, gifts, invitations, and notes such as "Mei patted your fox".
  • Reports: if you report someone, what you chose as the reason and where it happened.

Your phone also keeps a copy of your haven and your sign-in, so Haven works without a connection. If Haven closes by mistake, the phone keeps a short note of what went wrong and shows it to you the next time. It is not sent anywhere.

What Haven does not do

  • No ads, and no ad or tracking tools.
  • No analytics. Haven does not record how you use the app beyond the sessions above.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Who can see what

  • Your friends see your name, username, picture, level and haven, when you are focusing, and your sessions on the board and in the activity feed.
  • People in a sanctuary with you see your name, level and haven, and how long you focused this week for the great tree.
  • Anyone with your username or friend code can send you a friend request. They see your name and picture.
  • Whoever runs Haven can read the server's data to keep it working and to look at reports.

Services Haven uses

  • Cloudflare runs Haven's server and stores the data above. Like any server, it sees your network (IP) address when you connect. Haven uses it only to limit how often someone can try a password, and forgets it within an hour.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

All connections are encrypted (https).

How long it is kept

  • Your account, haven, finished sessions and friends: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: 30 days, or less once you have read them.
  • Sessions that were given up or cut short: 30 days.
  • Campfires: 2 days.
  • Reports: one year. A report about someone stays for that year even if they delete their account, with their name and username as they were.

Deleting your account

In Haven, open Settings and choose Delete account. Or use the account deletion page. Your account, haven, sessions, friends, messages and the reports you made are removed straight away and for good. Signing out of a phone also removes your haven from that phone.

Children

Haven is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly keep data about them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is put on this page with a new date. Big changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
